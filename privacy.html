Privacy

Short version: we keep your email and any survey answers you give, only to plan the Arc Lab launch and to email you about it. We never sell or share them. Ask, and we delete them.

What we keep

  • Your email (and your first name, if you give it), so we can write to you about the waitlist and the launch.
  • Your survey answers (the tier you'd pick, the price that feels fair, your series idea, your setup and how you heard about us), so we can set fair prices and decide what to build first.
  • Your consent to the launch emails, and when you gave it.

We look at the answers together, as totals ("most people picked Creator"). We never publish your name, email or series idea. If we'd like to quote your idea, we'll ask you first.

Who handles it

  • Tally (tally.so) runs the sign-up form on our page and stores the answers for us. Tally's own privacy policy covers what Tally itself logs when you use the form.
  • We may copy the answers into a private spreadsheet that only Arc Lab can open, to work out the prices.
  • When we email you, the email service we use handles your address only to deliver those emails.

This website

  • It has no cookies, ads, analytics or trackers of its own, and it never asks you to sign in.
  • It loads nothing from other sites, except the Tally form (and Tally's small script that fits it to the page) when you open the sign-up form. The video and pictures are stored on this site; the links to YouTube and Instagram only open those sites when you tap them.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses and browser details to keep the service secure. We don't use those logs.

Emails

You'll get a few emails before the launch (how the series was made, a free mini-lesson, the demo and the prices) and the launch email itself. To stop them, reply "stop" to any of them or email us. We take you off within 48 hours.

How long we keep it

We delete the survey answers 6 months after launch. Your email and name stay on the list until you ask us to remove them, or until we close the list.

Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete all of it, at any time. Email aviraorigins@gmail.com and we'll do it within 7 days. The waitlist is for people aged 16 and over.

Who we are

Arc Lab is a small independent studio. Questions about this page: aviraorigins@gmail.com.
