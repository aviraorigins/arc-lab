You're in.

Thank you for buying the Series Skill. Here's the short way from here to your first episode.

  1. Check your email

    Polar, our shop, sends your receipt with a link to your order page. If it isn't there in five minutes, look in spam or Promotions.

    The charge comes from Polar, so your card statement may show Polar's name rather than Arc Lab.

  2. Get your files and your help channel

    On your order page:

    • download the Series Skill zip, or connect GitHub for the private repository (best for updates);
    • join our Discord with the button there. You get the Owner role and the #help channel.

  3. Set it up in about 10 minutes

    The setup guide walks you through it, and Claude does most of the work.

    Open the setup guide

  4. Make episode 1

    One idea, three approvals: the words, the look and the draft. Share it in Discord when it's done; the best ones get featured on our page.

Founding member? Your email has the link to put your name on the Arc Lab founding wall.

Stuck, or changed your mind? Ask in #help, or email contact@arclabstudios.com. Refunds within 14 days, no questions asked.

In about a week we'll ask how episode 1 went. Every review goes on our page, good or bad.
