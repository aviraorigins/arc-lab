Questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The skill writes and runs the code. You make the creative calls and approve three times per episode.

What do I need?

Claude Code with a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max) or a pay-as-you-go Claude API key; Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+ with about 2 GB free; and an ElevenLabs account for the voices (a paid ElevenLabs plan if you'll publish commercially). No GPU, and nothing extra for music or sound effects. The setup guide shows every step before you buy.

Can I use it without a paid Claude plan?

Yes: instead of a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max), you can use a pay-as-you-go Claude API key. An episode takes a few hours of the skill working, so the usage adds up, and we haven't measured what one episode costs: set a spending limit in your API account and check your usage after your first episode. We only list setups we know work: other AI apps and free AI services aren't supported.

Do I need ElevenLabs?

Yes. This version can't make an episode without your ElevenLabs key: every episode is built around its narration, voiced with ElevenLabs stock voices. It's free to try, and you need a paid ElevenLabs plan if you'll publish commercially. We're testing a free voice that runs on your own computer, but it isn't in this version, so plan on ElevenLabs. If the free voice passes our tests, it will come as a free update. Music and sound effects never need ElevenLabs: the music is an original score made in code, and sound effects come from the skill's built-in library, plus free Pixabay sounds you can choose to fetch.

Is this AI video?

No. Our rendering code draws every frame: characters, sets, camera and lighting. No AI model makes the pictures. The narration uses stock voices from your own ElevenLabs account, with no voice cloning.

Is Your Trainer Arc made this way?

Yes. Every part was written, voiced, animated in code, mixed and checked using the same steps. The skill packages that process for your own original idea, and it contains no Pokémon characters, art or sounds.

How do I get it after paying?

Straight away. Polar emails your receipt, and your order page has the download, an invite to the private GitHub repo and the Discord link for help. Then the setup guide takes about 10 minutes.

Do I need GitHub?

No. The zip download works the same. A free GitHub account only makes updates a one-line command.

How long does an episode take?

Most of the time is the skill working: a few hours for a 30 to 40 second episode, mostly without you. Your part is the idea and three approvals.

Can I make money with what I make?

Yes, the original episodes you make are yours. The skill itself can't be shared or resold.

Reply to your receipt within 14 days and you get a full refund, no questions asked.
