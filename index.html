Questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The skill writes and runs the code. You make the creative calls and approve three times per episode.

What do I need?

A Windows or Mac computer, and Claude Code with a paid Claude plan or an API key. The voices can be free: one runs on your own computer. What it really takes has the details, and the setup guide shows every step before you buy.

Can I use it without a paid Claude plan?

Yes: instead of a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max), you can use a pay-as-you-go Claude API key. An episode takes a few hours of the skill working, so the usage adds up, and we haven't measured what one episode costs: set a spending limit in your API account and check your usage after your first episode. We only list setups we know work: for the Series Skill, other AI apps and free AI services aren't supported. No Claude Code at all? Start with the free playbook.

Do I need ElevenLabs?

No. Since version 1.1 the skill comes with a free voice that runs on your own computer: no key, no cost, a one-time download of about 350 MB. You can still use ElevenLabs on your own key (a paid plan if you'll publish commercially), or another voice service you already pay for. Music and sound effects never need a voice service.

Is this AI video?

No. Our rendering code draws every frame: characters, sets, camera and lighting. No AI model makes the pictures. The narration uses the free voice on your computer, or stock voices from your own ElevenLabs account, with no voice cloning.

How do I get it after paying?

Straight away. Polar emails your receipt, and your order page has the download, an invite to the private GitHub repo and the Discord link for help. Then the setup guide takes about 10 minutes.

How long does an episode take?

Most of the time is the skill working: a few hours for a 30 to 40 second episode, mostly without you. Your part is the idea and three approvals.

Can I make money with what I make?

Yes, the original episodes you make are yours. The skill itself can't be shared or resold.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email us within 14 days of buying. We'll help you get it running, and if we can't, you get a full refund. Bought at the 40% early-access price? Your 14-day refund still applies. The details.

Is there a local price where I live?

Yes, in many places. Checkout picks it from where you are: for example ₹999 in India, R$79 in Brazil, €22 in Europe and £18 in the UK, tax included. Paying from India? You can pay by UPI or by card.

What will my card statement say?

Polar, our shop, is the seller of record, so the charge may show Polar's name rather than Arc Lab.
