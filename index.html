The honest part.

You'll need
A paid Claude plan with Claude Code, a Windows or Mac computer, an ElevenLabs account for the voices, and a few hours of render time per episode.
You still decide
The idea, the three approvals and when to post. The skill does the heavy lifting; you stay the director.
It isn't a Pokémon kit
Your Trainer Arc is our unofficial fan series. The skill ships with original characters and contains no Pokémon characters, art or sounds.
No promised views
No one can honestly promise a hit. We can show you the format, the process and our own numbers.

Questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The skill writes and runs the code. You make the creative calls.

Is Your Trainer Arc made this way?

Yes. Every part was written, voiced, animated in code, mixed and checked using the same steps. The skill packages that process so it works for your own idea.

Is this AI video?

No. Everything is animated in code: characters, sets, camera and lighting. AI helps write the code and the voices, not the pictures.

How long does an episode take?

Most of the time is rendering, a few hours on a normal computer. Your part is the idea and three approvals.

Can I make money with what I make?

Yes, the original episodes you make are yours. The skill itself can't be resold.

What if it doesn't work for me?

You get a 14-day refund, no questions asked.

When does it launch?

We're finishing the skill now. Waitlist members get the date first, by email, with their 40% code.
