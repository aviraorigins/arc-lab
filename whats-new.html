What's new

We make our own series with the Series Skill every week, and what we learn goes back into it. Every v1 update is free for everyone who bought it.

When updates come

  • Fixes ship as soon as we've checked them.
  • New features, rules and set pieces ship on the last day of each month.

How to update

  • If you used GitHub: ask Claude to "update the Arc Lab skill". That's one git pull.
  • If you used the zip: download the new zip from your purchase (the link in your Polar email), and swap it in for the old skill folder. Then start a new Claude Code session and ask Claude to "run the Arc Lab setup check": swapping the folder removes the drawing library, and Claude puts it back.
  • Your series live in their own folders, so updating never touches your episodes.

1.1.0 "Free Hand", 14 November 2026

Our biggest update yet. Free for everyone who bought the skill, and your series folders aren't touched.

  • Real places, not backdrops. Outdoor scenes are built as real 3D places: shaded clouds, far mountains, hills, trees, winding paths, grass blade by blade, flowers, the sea, six times of day. The camera moves through them like a real camera, characters stand in the grass at their true size, and every shot of a place matches the others.
  • Episodes that come out right the first time. Claude now follows a step-by-step playbook with a check after every step: the script, the voice, the characters, every shot, the draft. A step only goes ahead when its check is clean, so the episode you see is already fixed.
  • Smarter stories. Every script is checked before you see it: a strong first second, a peak every 10-15 seconds, a breather between them, one answer, one new mystery, a step in your big secret, something funny, and an ending somewhere new. Narration in full, clear sentences. A season plan and an ideas library (hooks, fair twists, votes that split your audience, funny beats, ready episode shapes) come with every series.
  • A bigger band and more sounds. Thirteen new music instruments (drums, bass, marimba, plucked strings, a lead, stings, swells) for bright, playful scores as well as dark ones, and thirty-one new sound effects (comedy bonks and boings, whooshes, sparkles, impacts). All made in code: no downloads, no licences.
  • Votes that matter. Only the winning option happens. The vote card crosses out the others and shows what they cost, and every option is checked to make sense to someone who's never seen your series.
  • Elbows and hands that look right. Say where a hand goes (the chin, an ear, a pocket, the other shoulder) and the arm bends like a real one. Every character gets an arm check, and any broken arm is caught before the draft.
  • Shots in one line. Close-ups, two-shots, wides, from behind, long lenses, crash zooms: each framed for your format with faces clear of the captions.
  • Any frame, any length. Vertical 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube, or square 1:1 for feed posts. The captions, the vote, the covers and the safety checks fit each one. Long episodes are told in chapters.
  • A free voice. A voice that runs on your own computer: no key, no cost. Or keep ElevenLabs, or use another voice service you already pay for.
  • Autopilot. Say "autopilot" and Claude makes the whole episode, then shows you the script, the look and the draft at the end. Or keep the three approvals.
  • Your rating. Kid-safe, teen or mature: you choose it in the first talk.
  • Your call. Claude suggests original ideas, the ones you own, and leaves the final choice to you.
  • The Trainer Arc style pack. A ready look and world: a hoodie hero, eight original creature companions, battle screens, people, sets, props and eleven colour looks, all with model sheets, plus The Crossing, a story world to build your series on. Its first episode, "Six-Twelve", comes finished as an example.
  • A first win in minutes. Right after setup, the skill renders an example on your computer (Day 23, or the bright Trainer Arc one), so you see it working before your own idea.

1.0.1, 1 November 2026

  • Six written lessons, in the skill's lessons folder:
    1. install and check;
    2. your first idea;
    3. directing the script;
    4. voice and the look test;
    5. reading a draft and giving notes;
    6. posting, the vote, and the next episode.
  • Fixed on Mac: the setup check no longer says the engine link is wrong when it's right.
  • If you re-record a line after locking an episode, the captions and the sounds tied to words now follow the new take.
  • A clearer "You'll need" list: a paid Claude plan or a pay-as-you-go Claude API key, and Windows 10/11 or macOS 13 or newer.
  • Help links in the skill: the setup guide, #help on our Discord, and contact@arclabstudios.com.
