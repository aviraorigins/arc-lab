What's new

We make our own series with the Series Skill every week, and what we learn goes back into it. Every v1 update is free for everyone who bought it.

When updates come

  • Fixes ship as soon as we've checked them.
  • New features, rules and set pieces ship on the last day of each month.

How to update

  • If you used GitHub: ask Claude to "update the Arc Lab skill". That's one git pull.
  • If you used the zip: download the new zip from your purchase (the link in your Polar email), and swap it in for the old skill folder. Then start a new Claude Code session and ask Claude to "run the Arc Lab setup check": swapping the folder removes the drawing library, and Claude puts it back.
  • Your series live in their own folders, so updating never touches your episodes.

1.0.2, 1 November 2026

  • The Day 23 example now stops at episode 1. Day 23 is still running, so the example's notes hold only what episode 1 showed. Nothing in the engine changed, so this update is optional.

1.0.1, 1 November 2026

  • Six written lessons, in the skill's lessons folder:
    1. install and check;
    2. your first idea;
    3. directing the script;
    4. voice and the look test;
    5. reading a draft and giving notes;
    6. posting, the vote, and the next episode.
  • Fixed on Mac: the setup check no longer says the engine link is wrong when it's right.
  • If you re-record a line after locking an episode, the captions and the sounds tied to words now follow the new take.
  • A clearer "You'll need" list: a paid Claude plan or a pay-as-you-go Claude API key, and Windows 10/11 or macOS 13 or newer.
  • Help links in the skill: the setup guide, #help on our Discord, and contact@arclabstudios.com.
